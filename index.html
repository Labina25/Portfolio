01 / About

Good models. Clean pipelines.

I'm a BCA graduate who fell for the moment raw, messy data turns into something a model can actually learn from. Most of my work lives in Python: cleaning datasets, engineering features, and building regression pipelines with scikit-learn, then wrapping the result in a Flask app so people who've never opened a notebook can still use it.

Before I leaned fully into ML, I sat on the other side of software as a QA analyst at Deloitte USI, testing components, tracking bugs in JIRA, and writing the kind of structured reports that make sure nothing ships broken. That habit of asking does this actually work, and can I prove it? followed me straight into machine learning, where clean evaluation metrics matter as much as clever models.
